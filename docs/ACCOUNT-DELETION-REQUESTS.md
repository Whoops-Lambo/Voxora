# Account-deletion requests

Direct public URL: https://voxora-public.onrender.com/account-deletion

Use this URL for the account-deletion request field in Google Play Console. The page is unlisted in public navigation and sends `noindex, nofollow, noarchive` directives. It is publicly accessible to anyone with the URL, including people who cannot sign in.

The form requires an account username or user ID and confirmation that the request concerns the submitter’s own account. Contact email and additional details are optional. Passwords, payment data and identity documents should not be submitted. Requests use the existing rate-limited public form service and its private receipt mechanism.

Platform Admin → Reports → Report source → Account deletion requests shows submissions to staff with Review Platform Reports. The submitted identity and contact email are unverified. Staff must establish ownership and process the requested deletion through the operator’s account/data handling process. Updating or closing a review does not delete, suspend or sign out an account; this update adds a request intake flow, not an automatic account-erasure service.

Keep account-specific verification and outcomes private. Receipt lookup returns only the limited review status, never the request description, contact email or staff notes. Open requests remain for review; closed requests are retained for up to 90 days and may be removed earlier at storage limits. The privacy policy and request page explain these limits.
