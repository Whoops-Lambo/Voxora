Voxora 2.17.2 — working pings and external-drive updates

Type @ in the composer to search and select people or roles. Use arrow keys and Enter/Tab to insert a mention. Personal, role, @everyone and @here pings now feed highlights, mention badges, Inbox and notification preferences; thread pings open the right thread. Code, escaped text, links and copied announcements do not ping. Edits do not create fresh alerts.

Server Settings → Roles includes Allow anyone to mention this role. Other role and server-wide pings require Mention Everyone & Roles, including channel overrides. Only members who can view the channel are targeted; @here uses visible online presence. Mutes, blocked authors and Do Not Disturb remain effective.

Large update downloads now live in .voxora-updates beside the running app. External-drive installations keep downloads on their own drive. Free-space checks prevent downloading without room and prevent installation handoff from closing the app when its installation drive lacks space. Existing staging, rollback and exact-installer Windows verification remain in place.

If your Windows drive has very little free space, free at least 1–2 GB, save this installer directly to the external drive, close Voxora and select an external installation folder in setup. Launch Voxora.exe from that folder and check your shortcut points to it. Keep %APPDATA%\Voxora; your accounts, messages and preferences remain there. Installing another copy does not move the one your old shortcut opens.

Separately hosted servers need their source updated and Node service restarted for the new mention UI and backend. The desktop updater upgrades the bundled local workspace automatically.
