Voxora 3.0 — Your community, in motion

COMMUNITY STUDIO
Shared project boards turn conversations into plans. Create tasks from a message, assign members, set priorities/deadlines/labels, discuss details, and move work through To do, In progress and Done. Archive finished work, search/filter boards and see your assigned tasks in Home. Current channel permissions and versioned edits protect access and concurrent changes.

COMMUNITY GIVEAWAYS
Run free-entry giveaways with optional eligibility roles and 1–10 winners. Entries persist, duplicate requests do not add chances, and the running backend draws eligible winners automatically using cryptographic randomness. Saved draws cannot be rerun. Manage Giveaways controls creation, early draws, cancellation and closed history.

COMMUNITY INSIGHTS
View 7- or 30-day activity charts, visible-channel totals, active people, threads and completed tasks. View Community Insights permission is required, and private-channel activity remains hidden from viewers without access.

YOUR PRIVATE NOTEBOOK
Keep ideas and checklists in an account-private notebook. Pin and search pages, choose colors, export notes or local drafts, and continue on another signed-in device. Manual saves and revision checks preserve newer work when devices disagree. Platform staff cannot read another account’s notebook through the app.

HOME AND THE PLATFORM WEBSITE
Home brings assigned work and active community giveaways together alongside reminders, drafts, shortcuts and scheduled messages. Studio is available from navigation, server menus and sidebars. The public website introduces the new tools with the midnight navy and mint identity.

BOTS
The SDK now supports Studio projects, giveaways and insights. The starter ZIP includes an optional start-studio-bot.bat with /projects and /giveaways commands, while the existing economy/moderation template is preserved.

SAFE UPGRADES
External-drive update downloads, free-space preflight, staged installation, rollback and exact-installer Windows verification remain in place. Existing accounts, messages, roles, notes and saved connections stay with your backend. Installed apps discover the official feed automatically; Restart and Install remains your choice. A separately hosted backend must also be updated and restarted for 3.0 features. No domain is required for Local Workspace.
