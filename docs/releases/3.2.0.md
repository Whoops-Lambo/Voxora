Voxora 3.2 — Community Care

PRIVATE SUPPORT DESK
Open private support requests from the server sidebar or Community Studio. Staff can claim work, set priority, answer members and keep internal notes. Members see only their own conversations. Resolve, reopen, archive and search tickets, customize categories, and export transcripts that respect staff-note privacy. Draft replies survive incoming updates, and version checks protect competing work.

COMMUNITY KNOWLEDGE BASE
Turn repeated questions into useful guides. Write drafts with live previews, publish to a channel's audience, add summaries and tags, and pin guides in Knowledge Base and Home. Members can search and give helpfulness feedback. Ten saved editions and versioned restore make it easier to correct a guide without losing earlier work. Private-channel guides and drafts stay out of unauthorized searches and Home cards.

TIMED SAFETY CENTER
Pause new members, ordinary member chat, or both during a disruption. Choose a timed pause or stop it manually. Moderators can keep responding, members can still reach support, and roles/channel overrides remain intact. Regular/custom invites, Discovery and default-community registration respect join pauses. Members see the public reason; timed expiry restores access automatically across restarts.

HOME, STUDIO AND STAFF TOOLS
Home brings your support conversations and pinned community guides together with existing tasks, giveaways, reminders and notes. New role permissions separate ticket creation, private support management, knowledge editing and safety management. Server Settings links to the Safety Center.

BOTS
The SDK supports published knowledge search and article reads with installation/channel access checks. The optional Studio bot adds /knowledge alongside /projects and /giveaways. Private tickets, staff notes, drafts and revision history are excluded from bot access. The existing moderation/economy starter is preserved.

UPGRADES
Accounts, chats, role settings, profile pictures, custom invite links, boosts and saved server connections are preserved. The release uses the official update feed, external-drive download storage, installation-space preflight, staged replacement and rollback. Separately hosted backends still need the updated source, npm ci and a restart. No public hosting service is provisioned by this update.
