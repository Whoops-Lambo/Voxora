Voxora 3.2.1 — Public Voxora

New desktop installs connect directly to https://voxora-public.onrender.com. Register once on this service to share communities, friends and conversations across computers.

Existing saved connections continue to open as before. Press Alt and choose Voxora → Join Public Voxora, or use the new public-service button in Server Connection. Local accounts and messages stay on your PC and are separate from public accounts. Open Local Workspace remains available.

Connection setup shows the public address, offers retry after an unavailable service, and waits longer for a sleeping service to wake up. Failed shared connections never create a new private platform or owner. Installation identity checks remain in place.

Public deployment checks verify metadata, the chat page and sign-in requirements without creating accounts. Windows release checks exercise a real fresh desktop connecting to the public service, plus explicit local hosting and an upgrade from the published 3.2.0 installer.

The updater preserves external-drive download storage, space checks, staged installation, rollback and saved workspace data. Restart and Install remains your choice. Render backend updates remain a separate Manual Deploy operation.
