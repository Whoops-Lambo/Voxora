Submitting this form creates a private request. It does not immediately delete, suspend or sign out any account. Do not include passwords, recovery codes, payment details or identity documents.

Data and retention

Your request covers your account, profile, sign-in sessions and associated personal data. Staff will review related messages, uploads, server ownership and order records when handling the request. Any information that must be retained for legal, security or transaction requirements will be addressed during review.

Deleted server content can remain in server recovery for its retention window, up to 90 days. Closed request records are retained for up to 90 days and may be removed earlier if storage limits are reached; open requests remain available for review. Hosting logs and backups may have different retention periods. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

This form is for the Voxora service on this website. For an account on a separately operated Voxora server or private workspace, contact that operator.
