iPhone — install directly

  1. Open Voxora in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.

Launch Voxora from your Home Screen. No App Store submission, signing account or weekly renewal is needed for this web app.

iPhone — native sideload

Download iOS IPA

This native build must be signed for your device using an Apple ID through AltStore Classic or Sideloadly on your computer. A free Apple ID generally needs renewal every seven days. Downloading the IPA alone does not install it.

Chat and calls require an internet connection. Sounds and live updates work while Voxora is running; closed-app push notifications are not included.

Keep the public account you already use. Your old local desktop workspace remains separate.
