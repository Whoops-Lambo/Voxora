Privacy Policy

Understand what Voxora stores, how reports work and the choices you have.

1. About this policy

This policy describes information handled by the Voxora service on this website and its connected clients. Operators of other Voxora installations control their own deployments and may have additional privacy notices. The Safety Center on this website sends reports to authorized staff for this service.

2. Accounts and conversations

The service stores account information, password hashes, sessions, preferences, relationships, community memberships, messages, files and moderation records needed to run its features. Private means access is restricted by the service’s permissions; conversations are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized operators with access to the backend storage can access stored data. HTTPS protects data in transit when you use the public HTTPS service.

3. Anonymous Safety Center reports

No sign-in, reporter name or email is required. The reporting endpoint does not attach a signed-in account, session or user-agent to a report. Reports store the member identifier, category, context, description and links you submit, their status and staff review records. Do not include identifying information about yourself if you want to stay anonymous. Your description or links may still reveal your identity. Hosting providers and network services may maintain connection logs, so complete anonymity cannot be guaranteed.

4. Abuse prevention and receipts

Temporary network-derived keys, form tokens and rate-limit counters are kept in server memory to limit spam and do not become part of the saved report. The service stores a hash of your private receipt key. Your copy is shown after submission; it is not placed in a URL or saved automatically in browser storage. If you choose to copy or download it, protect that copy. Anyone with the reference and key can see only the report’s limited review status.

5. Who can see a report

Only the platform owner and staff with Review Platform Reports can read anonymous Safety Center reports. The reported member and ordinary server moderators do not automatically receive them. Website reports contain information you choose to submit; they do not grant staff arbitrary access to private channel history. In-app server reports follow channel permissions and may also be explicitly shared with Voxora staff.

6. How long information is kept

Open reports are kept for review. Closed anonymous reports are automatically removed after 90 days and may be removed earlier if storage limits are reached. Their receipt then stops working. Other account content, server reports and audit records follow the service’s feature-specific storage and deletion behavior. Backups and hosting logs may have different retention practices. A source-code update does not delete existing chat history.

7. Information on your device

Voxora uses session cookies for sign-in and device storage for preferences and drafts. The anonymous report form does not use your sign-in cookie or save report drafts automatically. The install service worker caches its offline page and public icon, not message history. If you enable push, the server stores your browser subscription and preferences, bound to your signed-in session. A single account ID is kept in the device notification worker to prevent cross-account alerts. Push providers carry encrypted notification payloads; your operating system displays them. Message previews are off by default. Logging out removes the session’s push subscriptions. Microphone and camera access is requested for calls you choose to join. You can control permissions in your device or browser settings.

8. Payments and other providers

Stripe processes payments where purchases are enabled. Voxora stores subscription and account-binding information needed to apply benefits, not payment-card numbers. Third-party bots, linked websites, Apple/Google software and hosting providers handle information under their own practices. This website does not ask you to submit Apple ID passwords, bot tokens or payment credentials through the Safety Center.

9. Your choices and requests

You can choose what to share, block unwanted users, mute conversations and change available profile or notification settings. Use the Safety Center privacy category for questions or requests about information handled by this service. Do not include passwords or sensitive credentials. Some account-specific requests require identity verification. Mandatory privacy rights under applicable law continue to apply.

10. Updates and contact

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date and any notice required by applicable law. Contact the team operating this service through the Safety Center.
