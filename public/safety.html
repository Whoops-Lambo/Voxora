A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Before you report

Will the member know who reported them?

This form does not collect a reporter account, name or email. Reports and evidence are available only to authorized platform reviewers, not the reported member. Avoid writing identifying details if you want to stay anonymous.

What can Voxora staff review?

Staff review the information and links you submit. A report does not grant arbitrary access to private channel history. Add relevant evidence; do not share unlawful material or someone’s private information unnecessarily.

How long does a review take?

Review times vary with the concern and information available. Save your receipt and use the status checker. Do not rely on this form for urgent emergency help.

Can I report a moderator or a verified server member?

Yes. Platform staff review anonymous reports independently of ordinary server roles or verification badges. Include the member’s username or ID and relevant context.
